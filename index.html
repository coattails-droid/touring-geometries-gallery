TOURING GEOMETRIES

volumetric · 4 tokens · 360px loops · 16s orbit, camera 5.625° below center level

Each token sings — and each token has a heartbeat. Its accent-dot field is a generative sequencer in D major pentatonic at A=432Hz: the hash writes the score, and a heart rate (60, 82.5, or 105 BPM) swells every pulse. On-chain the music is synthesized live in your browser — open a live token and tap to unmute.

The motion is a 16-second orbit — a full 360° circle at 5.625° below center level. A fold wave sweeps through twice per loop, the twin core spindles fountain and ignite at each fold peak, and Fibonacci shells ripple from the two foci. The end lands exactly where the beginning was.

From flat to volume. Touring grew out of the Tidal Geometries 2D series — same hash family, same trait DNA, extruded into depth: the rings become shells, the grid becomes a 3D lattice, the accent dots become a blinking star field.
